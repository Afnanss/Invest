01 / THE PROBLEM

Meet Afnan.
She spends.
She saves.
But she doesn't invest.

Not because she doesn't care about her future. Investing feels complicated, time-consuming, and out of reach. Yet she uses her bank card every single day.

☕
Daily purchasesGroceries · Coffee · Shopping
Investment balanceNot started yet
02 / THE SOLUTION

Same spending.
Instead of cashback, investments.

Afnan uses Invest+ like any debit card linked to her current account. The difference is simple: instead of receiving cashback in cash, her cashback value becomes an investment through SNB Capital, after the required setup.

Afnan does not invest part of the purchase price. Her purchase is paid normally; the reward is a separate benefit.

HOW CASHBACK BECOMES AN INVESTMENT

Small cashback today.
An investment tomorrow.

Sometimes Afnan earns just 50 halalas, sometimes two riyals. She doesn't need to invest every tiny amount separately. Cashback collects in a dedicated balance, and once it reaches SAR 100, it is sent to her chosen investment.

The SAR 100 threshold is a proposal for this concept. Actual minimum subscriptions, processing costs and settlement arrangements require validation with SNB Capital.

07 / THE BUSINESS MODEL

Good for Afnan.
Designed to create value for the group.

The commercial opportunity goes beyond a cashback transaction. Each participant has a distinct role — and a distinct source of value.

💳

SNB

Potential net payment revenue, incremental card usage and stronger engagement. The base reward must fit within actual debit-card economics.

🤝

Merchant partners

Fund targeted bonus rewards to attract measurable spending, reducing the amount of promotional reward cost borne by SNB.

📊

SNB Capital

Potential new investment relationships, optional recurring contributions and fee-generating assets under management, subject to fund terms.

05 / THE MOMENT OF TRUST

Afnan feels
at ease.

Afnan feels reassured. Small cashback amounts no longer get lost in everyday spending. They build up to SAR 100, then are invested through SNB Capital. She chooses a suitable conservative option and leaves the investment management to professionals.

“I just want to spend as usual, while feeling that I'm doing something for my future. And I like knowing professionals manage my investment.”

Afnan chooses a conservative investment preference. She reviews the fund's risks and suitability, knowing that conservative does not mean risk-free or guaranteed.

Invest+ proposes a collaboration between SNB (card and payments) and SNB Capital (investment product and management). A partnership does not automatically imply SAMA approval; implementation requires review of SAMA, CMA and compliance requirements and any necessary approvals.

06 / ONE SMALL DECISION, EVERY MONTH

“I'll add
SAR 2,000
every month.”

Cashback alone grows slowly, so Afnan thinks: “Why not add some of my own money?” She authorizes SAR 2,000 to be transferred monthly from her salary account into her investment. Her cashback keeps building, and her own contribution is added automatically.

No extra effort each month. She can adjust or pause future contributions according to the service terms.

This SAR 2,000 is Afnan's own money, separate from her earned rewards. Investment values may rise or fall; no capital protection or return is promised.
